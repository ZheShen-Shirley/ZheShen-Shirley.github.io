Solo travelers around the world. I am always excited by the people I meet and the cultures I experience on my trips. They show me different possibilities in life, which I find amazing. I am also constantly touched by the small acts of kindness between people. 

- 54H California Zephyr from Chicago to San Francisco
  
I met a lady from San Jose. She seemed to have studied sociology in Europe, and we talked about movies and our experiences there. What surprised me when we discussed religion was that she seems to have read a lot of books about the Dao. She is not a typical believer. Instead, she collects pieces of wisdom from all religions that resonate with her. It is a very interesting philosophy that we shouldn't believe anything blindly. We learn, collect, and discard. What remains is who we are. Because we collect different parts, we become different people.
